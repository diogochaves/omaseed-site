# Research behind the direction (2026-10-14)

## The three reference sites (read in full)
- **Raycast** (raycast.com): floating translucent bar, one light Download pill; one-line promise;
  an accurate app window in a macOS frame with a row of five buttons under it that change the
  content; two-tone headlines (white, then dim grey); a big keyboard with four labelled keys
  (Fast, Ergonomic, Personal, Reliable); glowing per-extension cards; a wall of named users;
  a closing "Download" call with a Homebrew line. Too loud for us: the fixed red neon.
- **Linear** (linear.app): same bar and pill; a two-line headline; a real issue view with an
  animated chat panel; logos strip; a statement paragraph (two-tone); three blocks numbered
  "FIG 0.1 to 0.3" with thin isometric line drawings; feature sections of a large title, one
  sentence and a product slice; a dated changelog block on the home page; a final call to action.
- **herdr.dev**: the page contains a replica of the app ("the herd, from a client that isn't there",
  "click an agent"), numbered sections with a tiny live widget on the right of each, a stats row
  (stars, installs), an Ink/Paper toggle, a one-line install command with a copy button.

Other sites looked at and set aside: Ghostty (hero is only the app window), Zed (screenshot grid),
Omarchy.org (pixel type, green; same ecosystem), Sublime Text (theme switch above the screenshot,
dated gradient), and Landbook's recent feed (Replicas, Kyoso, Ardor, Pip, Ghost AI). Awwwards
Sites of the Day are agency portfolios, not app pages.

## Free reference galleries
Landbook (land-book.com), Awwwards, Godly (redirects to recent.design), Minimal Gallery,
Component Gallery, Navbar Gallery, Footer Design, CTA Gallery, 404s, AppShot Gallery, ui-skills.com
(also lists real Design.md files from Vercel, Clerk, Mintlify and others), Pinterest.
Not all were opened; only Landbook, Awwwards and ui-skills.com were read.

## Design-skill ecosystem (from replies on X)
- Impeccable (impeccable.style): `/impeccable polish`, keeps a DESIGN.md, strips "AI tells", has a
  detector and a live mode.
- Taste Skill (tasteskill.dev): 13 SKILL.md files; `npx skills add Leonxlnx/taste-skill`.
- ui-skills.com: a catalog and CLI listing Impeccable, Anthropic's frontend-design, better-ui and more.
- Mobbin MCP (paid; one reply says about $45 minimum) and Paper MCP: reference-driven redesign.
- Consensus in the replies: rules alone give generic output; real references, a written design
  system and a screenshot critique loop give good output; "aggressive deletes" and taste matter most.
- revenue-centric-design is about conversion and pricing, not visuals: not for omaseed.
