# What the v0.6.0 window replicas are drawn from (2026-11-05)

One read-only pass over `~/code/omarchy/omaseed` at tag `v0.6.0` (HEAD `e7050c60`, the checksum
commit after it; CHANGELOG "## v0.4.0" to "## v0.6.0" is what shipped, the boards are proposals).
Where a board or DECISIONS and the code at the tag disagree, these notes follow the code and say
so (**code wins**). Anchored on the visual check's runs of 2026-11-04 (one in the Omarchy skin, one in Console; 45 screens each). Example names are the app's own test fixtures (server "box", `distro-2026.09-
x86_64.iso`, `Field Recordings Vol. 3 [FLAC]`, `/home/sbx/Torrents`) or made up. Re-check against
omaseed before changing a replica; this is a record, not a source of truth.

Colour words are Omarchy theme keys as the QML names them: `accent`, `foreground`,
`brightForeground`, `dimForeground`, `background`, `red`, `yellow`, `green`, plus three the state
colours use: `blue` (downloading), `magenta` (queued, the table's "private"), `orange` (stalled).
Fills are `fill(alpha)` of the foreground: idle 4 %, hover/cursor 8 %, selected 18 %, pressed
22 %, selected under the cursor 24 %; hairline 12 %; faint 40 %. Text on a fill is lifted to
4.5:1 (C30), so a replica's dim words on a selected row read a little brighter than on the window.

**Sizes.** The code's numbers are spec px at base 12 (`Theme.space(n)` = n × base / 12; the
screenshots are base 12, so their px are spec px). At base 14 multiply by 7/6; the base-14 value
is given in brackets: `200 [233]`. Fonts at base 14: caption 12, small 13, body 14, subtitle 15,
title 16, heading 19 (at base 12: 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16). Control height = body × 2.333 (33 at
base 14), rail row = body × 2.1 (29), table row = body × 2.4 (34), status line = base × 2.2 (31).
Hairlines and the 2 px lit lines are px as written, not scaled.

## 1. The rail

**Shape.** Left column, open `200 [233]` wide (Console `222 [259]`), folded `46 [54]` (Console
`52 [61]`). Its edge drags between `160 [187]` and `360 [420]`; let go under `120 [140]` and it
folds; while dragging a small label reads `↔ 214` (the width) or `fold` (accent). Double-click
the edge: back to the default width. It folds by itself when the list would get narrower than
`520 [607]` (the open details pane counted) and unfolds when there is room, unless folded or
unfolded by hand. **Code wins** over C32's "back to 214": 214 was board 51's px at 13 px type;
the code's default is `Theme.railWidth`, 200 spec.

**The fold tab** (C32 D9 c, moved to the top): a small `background` square with a 1 px `accent`
edge on the rail's right edge, centred on the title bar's height, shown while the pointer is on
the rail: `‹` open, `›` folded. Tooltip `fold the rail · ctrl+b` / `unfold the rail · ctrl+b`.

**Head (the mark):** the title bar's height, `46 [54]` (Console `58 [68]`), a hairline under it.
The omaseed mark (`icon:mark`, `20 [23]`) at 14 in, gap 9, then `omaseed` (body, DemiBold,
`brightForeground`) and a small dim `▾`. A click (or `ctrl+.`) opens the omaseed menu (§6); while
open the mark is lit (the selected fill) and the menu grows from it. Accessible name `omaseed
menu`. Folded: the mark alone.

**Rows** (Kit/RailRow): `29` tall at base 14, words `16 [19]` in, glyph column `14 [16]` (small
size), gap `8 [9]`, count right-aligned `16 [19]` from the edge, tabular, `dimForeground`. Name
in body: `foreground`; `brightForeground` when selected or current.
- A **filter row picked** (a question, a category, a folder…): the selected fill (18 %), glyph
  `accent`, name bright.
- The **view on show** (library, seed, log, search): the hover fill (8 %) plus a 2 px `accent`
  rule down its left edge (Console: bracket corners instead, §12), so a view and a filter never
  look alike.
- Group headings (RailGroup): caption size, upper case, tracked, `dimForeground`, `16 [19]` in,
  4 above and 4 below; click folds the group. Count, when shown, at the right in `dimForeground`.

**Order, top to bottom:**
1. Views (no heading), padding 8 above: `≡ library` `0` · `↑ seed` `` ` `` · `~ log` `~` ·
   `⌕ search` `ctrl+f` (search only on a qBittorrent server). Library shows the total count.
2. `now`: the five questions, keys `1`–`5`:
   `! needs you` · `↓ arriving` · `✓ landed today` · `↑ giving back` · `■ resting`.
   Glyphs `dimForeground` (`accent` when picked). **Needs you with anything in it:** glyph and
   count `red`. Counts dim otherwise, `0` shown.
3. `categories`: `all` (selected by default), `none` (hidden at 0 when real ones exist), then
   each category (glyph `icon:folder`). Collapses to `all` alone with no categories.
4. `folders`: a tree of save paths. A drive's top is dim with a fold glyph (`▾`/`▸`), e.g.
   `▾ /home/sbx`; folders under it indent `12 [14]` per level with a `·` glyph, e.g.
   `· Torrents  9`. Right-click a folder: "move this folder…", "create a torrent from a folder in
   it…". (C15/C19/C27.)
5. `tags`: `all`, `untagged` (hidden at 0 when real ones exist), each tag (`#`).
6. `trackers`: `all`, each host with `◦` (a host elides at its start).
7. **Servers block, only with 2 or more servers** (`serverCount > 1`), above the foot, a
   hairline over it: heading `servers · 2` (Console: `SERVERS サーバー` with the count at the
   right), then a row per server: a dot (`green` the current one answering, `dimForeground`
   others, `yellow` retrying/first sync, `red` refused) and its name, the torrent count at the
   right (or a word like `down`). The current one has the selected fill. Past the room:
   `N more · ctrl+s`. Folded it is one line: the current server's dot, `▴`, `N servers`.
   One server: no block at all; the status line names it (§8).
8. **Foot:** a hairline, then `⚙ settings` (key `,`), always at the very bottom. Folded: the gear
   alone.

**Folded rail:** the view glyphs, the five question glyphs, one `▤` row standing for
"categories, folders, tags, trackers" (lit while one of them narrows the library; a click
unfolds), the gear. Names go into tooltips.

**Right-click a server** (Views/ServerMenu, width `376 [439]`). Head: the name (bright) over
`127.0.0.1:8080 · qBittorrent 5.3.0 · 9 torrents` (another server: `last seen …` instead of the
version). Rows:
- On the current server: `✓ the current server` (dimmed, disabled) · the alternative speeds row
  (its words follow the set: `turtle`, `burst · until 15:30`, else `alternative speed
  limits`; key `A`) · `stop all` · `start all`.
- On another server: `→ switch to it` with `enter` as its note.
- Then, both: separator · `edit…` · `test the connection` · `open its web UI` · `copy its
  address` · separator · `remove…` (red; asks first).

**Surface tint** (Settings › Appearance › Surface tint: off · 4 % · 8 %): on the rail only.

On **log** the rail holds the log's four levels (normal, info, warning, critical) ticked with
counts (`1`–`4`), the torrents its lines are about, and since when; "+ add ▾" becomes
`export log…`. On **search** it holds §10's groups.

## 2. The title bar

**Shape.** Across the content beside the rail's head, the same height `46 [54]` (Console
`58 [68]`), insets `16 [19]` start / `13 [15]` end, gap `11 [13]` between parts, a hairline under.
Left: the title (title size 16, DemiBold, `brightForeground`) over its sentence (small 13,
`dimForeground`), 1 px between. Right, in order: the two speeds, (a folder's `move this
folder…`), the filter field, the display tool, `+ add ▾`. Tools are kit buttons, control height
(`33` at base 14), outlined; `+ add ▾` has the `accent` outline and accent words. The
title and sentence elide first; narrow steps at `760 [887]` and `560 [653]` drop the tools'
words (icon only) and give the speeds back to the status line.

**Title:** `Library` · a question's name with a capital (`Needs you`, `Arriving`, `Landed
today`, `Giving back`, `Resting`) · a category's name (`No category`) · a tag (`Untagged`) · a
tracker host · a folder's last part (`Torrents`) · `Search` · `Log` · `Seed console`.

**Sentence** (core/ScopeSentence; parts joined by ` · `):
- Library: as many of these as apply, in order: `1 needs you` / `2 need you` · `7 arriving,
  the next in 3 min` (no estimate: `7 arriving`) · `2 landed today` · `giving back 41 KB/s`
  (nothing sending: `3 giving back`); none of those: `9 resting`. Empty library: `no torrents
  yet`. Example the app itself shows: `2 need you · 7 arriving, the next in 3 min · 2 landed
  today · giving back 41 KB/s`.
- Needs you: `1 torrent waits for a fix · nothing else is wrong` (`2 torrents wait for a fix`);
  empty `nothing needs you`.
- Arriving: `7 torrents · the next ready in 3 min, the last in about 2 h · 4.1 MB/s`; one:
  `1 torrent · ready in 3 min`; no estimate: `waiting for a seed` / `all waiting for a seed` /
  `2 waiting for a seed`; empty `nothing arriving`.
- Landed today: `3 since midnight · all seeding` (`both seeding`, `none seeding`, `2 seeding`;
  one: `seeding` / `stopped`); empty `nothing landed yet today`.
- Giving back: `5 torrents at 41 KB/s · ratio 1.6` (nobody: `5 torrents · nobody asking now`);
  empty `nothing giving back`.
- Resting: `9 torrents · not sharing`; empty `nothing resting`.
- A folder: `9 torrents · 1.46 GB on disk · 2 save paths · 3 loose files · /home/sbx/Torrents`
  (save paths and loose files only when there are). A folder adds the `move this folder…` tool
  (key `shift+m`).
- A category: `4 torrents · 1.2 GB · saves to /path`; a tag: `4 torrents · tag`; a tracker:
  `4 torrents · tracker`.
- Log: `qBittorrent's log on box · 120 lines since 02:30 · 2 warnings, 1 critical ·
  following`.
- Durations: under a minute `42 s`, under an hour `3 min` (rounded up), else `2 h 10 min` style.

**The speeds** (C32 D2 a: here by default; Settings › Appearance › Speeds puts them in the status
line): two figures, `↓` then `↑`. Each: the arrow (body, `blue` for ↓, `green` for ↑, `accent`
on hover), 5 px, the value (title 16, Medium, `brightForeground`, tabular), the unit (caption,
`dimForeground`; `0` has no unit: `↓ 0`). Under each, its last minute as a sparkline
`72×12 [84×14]`, a line in the arrow's colour, no fill. With turtle or burst on, the cap follows
the value in the set's colour (turtle `yellow`, burst `green`): `↓ 290 / 300 KB/s`. ↑ is a link
to the seed console (tooltip `seed console · `` ` `` plus today's line).

**The filter field:** `164 [191]` wide shut (Console `188 [219]`): `/ filter` dim, the count at
its right (`9`). Open (caret in it, or a filter set) it takes the bar's free room, the title
kept and its sentence gone; placeholder `words, or is:stalled, cat:films, tag:keep, tracker:…`;
tokens like `cat:` show as chips; ends in `× esc` (something typed) or `esc`. Key `/`.

**The display tool:** glyph + words + `▾`: `≡ rows · added ▾`, `▤ table · download speed ▾`,
`▦ space ▾` (space has no sort word). Tooltip `display: table, sorted by download speed`. No
key. Its menu (width `240 [280]`):
- `≡ rows` note `by question` · `▤ table` note `one sorted list` · `▦ space` note `by size`
  (the current one checked).
- separator, caption `sort, inside each group` (table: `sort`), radio rows with the direction as
  the picked row's note: `added` (`oldest first` / `newest first`) · `name` (`a to z` / `z to a`)
  · `size` (`smallest first` / `largest first`) · `progress` (`least done first` / `most done
  first`) · `time to ready` (`soonest first` / `latest first`) · `ratio` (`lowest first` /
  `highest first`). A table header sort not in the list shows as its own row (`download speed`).
- separator, `⇅ reverse`. Footer `also s, the sort palette`.
- On space: only the three displays; footer `size orders the map, the largest first`.

**`+ add ▾`** (key `a`), menu width `330 [385]`: `+ add a torrent or magnet…` `a` ·
`◇ create a torrent from a folder…` `ctrl+n` (qBittorrent Web API 2.10.4 or later only) ·
separator · `⌕ search…` `ctrl+f`. On log the tool is `export log…` instead.

**Bands** (a server's notice, a move, a creation) sit under the title bar across the content
only, the rail beside them usable; Omarchy's run edge to edge with a 3 px rule in their colour.

## 3. Rows, table, space

  the keys; selected: the 18 % fill with a 2 px `accent` rule at the start.

  - magnet: fact `finding files`, caption `MAGNET`, sentence `asking 4 peers for the file list`

  - checking: fact `checking`, caption `48.9 %`, sentence `making sure the files on disk are

  - queued: fact `next` or `queued`, caption `IN LINE`, sentence `starts when a download slot

  - moving: fact `moving`, caption the %, sentence `qBittorrent is moving its files`.

## 13. Words the site must not claim, or must word carefully

- **Install, quoted from README "Arch / Omarchy":** `curl -fLO
  https://github.com/diogochaves/omaseed/releases/latest/download/omaseed-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst &&
  sudo pacman -U omaseed-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst`, and "omaseed is not in a repo yet, so `pacman -Syu`
  does not update it: for a new release, run the same line again." It is **not** in Omarchy's
  `[omarchy]` repo: RELEASING step 7 only prints the steps for an `omacom/omarchy-pkgs` PR. No
  "in the Omarchy repo", no "`pacman -S omaseed`". Build from source: `git clone
  https://github.com/diogochaves/omaseed.git`, `cd omaseed/packaging/arch`, `makepkg -si`.
  Needs qBittorrent 5.2 or newer with its Web UI on.
- **qBittorrent only.** No engine of its own. Transmission and Deluge servers in `servers.json`
  are listed and never contacted (v0.4.0, "ready for other engines"): engines are a roadmap item,
  not a feature.
- **Search** only through the user's own qBittorrent search plugins on that server; omaseed names,
  ships, suggests and scrapes none. Results come from one server, the current one. The search row
  is in the rail on any qBittorrent server; with no plugins the view is the empty state (README's
  "isn't offered on a server without plugins to run" is looser than the code: code wins).
  Installing from a file only when the server is this computer.
- **Create** needs qBittorrent Web API 2.10.4 or later (no entry below it). A private torrent is
  saved, not added; omaseed never stores, shows or logs the passkey.
- **Turtle and burst** are one qBittorrent set used two ways; burst is omaseed writing over
  turtle while it runs. Don't say qBittorrent has a burst mode. The schedule is qBittorrent's.
  Burst lengths are 30 min, 1 h, until I stop it.
- **Recent searches** are this session only, in memory, never saved.
- **Skins:** two, Omarchy and Console; user skins are not in v0.6.0 ("later", C31). Console's
  fonts are optional packages (two from the AUR: `ttf-chakra-petch`, `ttf-share-gf`; plus
  `noto-fonts-cjk`, `ttf-material-symbols-variable`); without them it falls back to the system's.
- **Opaque by default** since v0.5.0 (Settings › Appearance › Window keeps "translucent"). The
  README's window-rule paragraph still says omaseed "paints its own translucent background": don't
  copy that onto the site.
- **Known limits** listed at v0.5.0: the action bar was see-through (fixed in v0.5.1); a Settings
  freeze was listed and v0.6.0 says it was already fixed in v0.5.0. v0.6.0 lists none of its own.
- **Not on the page until measured** (DIRECTION): memory, CPU, user counts.
- v0.4.0's outcome strip, row marks and mouse ticks have shipped (they were held back from the
  v0.3.0 page): they may go on the page now.
